# Trailhead Supply Co. — Exchanges, Cancellations & Order Changes

_Last updated: July 2026_

## Cancellations and Order Edits

An order may be cancelled or edited only while its order status is **Processing**.
Processing normally lasts 1–2 business days, but the available window can be shorter.

- Verify the order record before telling a customer that cancellation is available.
- Once an order is Shipped, In Transit, Out for Delivery, or Delivered, it cannot be
  cancelled or rerouted by support.
- Address changes are allowed only during Processing and require customer verification.
- A cancellation request is not a completed cancellation. The support API in this
  example is read-only, so it must say that a team member or connected order-management
  action is still required.
- Payment authorizations can remain visible for 3–7 business days after a cancellation.
  This is not necessarily a completed charge.

## Size and Color Exchanges

Eligible items can be exchanged during the same return window: **30 days from
delivery**, or **60 days for Trail Club members**.

- The item must be unused, with tags and original packaging.
- Footwear may be tried on indoors but cannot have outdoor wear.
- Final Sale items cannot be exchanged unless the wrong item was shipped. Manufacturing
  defects are handled under the Warranty Policy.
- Replacement inventory is not reserved until the return is accepted. If the desired
  variant is out of stock, offer a standard return instead.
- When prices differ, the original item is refunded and the replacement is purchased
  at its current price. Discount eligibility is reevaluated for the replacement order.

## Wrong Item or Missing Item

When Trailhead Supply Co. shipped the wrong item or omitted a line item:

1. Verify the order and item record.
2. Ask the customer to keep the packaging and provide a photo of the item or packing
   slip when available.
3. Escalate for fulfillment review and a prepaid label or replacement.
4. Original shipping charges are refundable when the error is ours.

Do not promise a replacement or refund before the fulfillment review confirms the
discrepancy.
