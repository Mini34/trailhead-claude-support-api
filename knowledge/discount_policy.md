# Trailhead Supply Co. — Discounts, Promotions & Price Adjustments

_Last updated: July 2026_

This policy is the authoritative source for student, educator, first responder,
promotional-code, and price-match questions. A case study can illustrate this policy,
but it cannot override it.

## Verified Student Discount — STUDENT15

Currently enrolled college and university students receive **15% off eligible
full-price merchandise** after verification.

- Verification is completed through the verification link in the customer's account.
- An approved verification is valid for **365 days**.
- The discount can be used **once every 30 days**. The 30-day period starts on the date
  of the order where the discount was used.
- The discount applies to eligible merchandise before tax. It does not reduce shipping
  charges.
- **Final Sale, clearance, gift cards, prior purchases, and shipping charges are
  excluded.**
- STUDENT15 cannot be combined with another promotion, an educator or first responder
  discount, or a price match.
- It can be combined with a customer's normal **Trail Club free Standard shipping**
  benefit because free shipping is a membership benefit, not a merchandise promotion.
- Support may explain eligibility and help with verification. Support must not invent
  or manually issue a verification approval.

If verification is pending, expired, or missing, direct the customer to reverify. Do
not ask the customer to email a student ID, transcript, class schedule, or other
sensitive document to support.

## Educator Appreciation — EDUCATOR10

Verified current K–12 and post-secondary educators receive **10% off eligible
full-price merchandise**. Verification lasts 365 days, the benefit can be used once
every 30 days, and the exclusions and stacking rules are the same as STUDENT15.

## First Responder — RESPONDER10

Verified EMTs, paramedics, firefighters, and law-enforcement personnel receive **10%
off eligible full-price merchandise**. Verification lasts 365 days, the benefit can be
used once every 30 days, and the exclusions and stacking rules are the same as
STUDENT15.

## Promotional Codes

- Only **one merchandise promotion** may be applied to an order.
- A code must be entered before checkout. Support cannot retroactively attach an
  expired code.
- If a valid code failed because of a confirmed site error, collect the code and order
  number and escalate for a manual review. Do not promise an adjustment.
- Percentage discounts apply only to eligible line items, not the entire order when an
  excluded item is present.

## Price Matching

Trailhead Supply Co. will match the advertised price of the identical in-stock item
from an authorized retailer within **14 days of purchase**.

- The item must have the same brand, model, size, and color.
- Marketplace, auction, membership-only, liquidation, bundle, used, refurbished, and
  typographical-error prices are excluded.
- Price matches do not combine with STUDENT15, EDUCATOR10, RESPONDER10, or promotional
  codes. The customer receives the single better merchandise discount.
- Ask for the competitor's public product link. Never request payment-card details.
- A price-match refund goes to the original payment method after review. The bot must
  not claim the refund has been issued.

## Discount Troubleshooting

Before escalating, check:

1. The customer's verification status and expiration date.
2. The last-used date and 30-day cooldown.
3. Whether every requested item is full-price and not Final Sale.
4. Whether another code or price match is already applied.
5. Whether the customer is signed in to the verified account.

Escalate unexplained technical failures with the order number or cart details, the
program code, and the exact error message. Never expose internal customer identifiers
or another customer's eligibility record.
