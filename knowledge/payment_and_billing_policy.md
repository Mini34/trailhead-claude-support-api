# Trailhead Supply Co. — Payments & Billing Support

_Last updated: July 2026_

## Authorization and Capture

Cards are authorized at checkout and normally charged when the order ships. Banks may
display an authorization as "pending." A pending authorization is not always a settled
charge.

- A released or cancelled authorization can take **3–7 business days** to disappear,
  depending on the bank.
- Trailhead Supply Co. cannot accelerate a bank's pending-authorization timeline.
- Refunds are sent to the original payment method and normally appear within **5–7
  business days** after processing.

## Duplicate Charge Reports

Duplicate-charge reports require a human billing review.

1. Verify the order number and customer account.
2. Ask whether both entries are settled or whether one is pending.
3. Collect only the transaction date, amount, and last four card digits if a billing
   specialist requests them through the secure support flow.
4. Never ask for a full card number, security code, bank password, or one-time code.
5. Do not promise that a refund has been issued.

## Failed Payments

Support can suggest:

- Confirming the billing address and postal code.
- Trying the card again once, or using another supported payment method.
- Contacting the card issuer when the issuer declines the payment.

Repeated attempts can create multiple pending authorizations. Do not advise repeated
rapid retries.

## Chargebacks, Fraud, and Account Takeover

Immediately escalate a chargeback threat, an unrecognized order, suspected fraud, or
an account-takeover report. Preserve the customer's words, minimize collection of
sensitive data, and do not accuse the customer or another person of fraud.
